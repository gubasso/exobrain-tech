# Glossary

One descriptive sentence per planning term, each naming the chapter whose rules govern it.

- `appetite` — the fixed budget a unit of work is bounded by, chosen before its design. Rules:
  [01 — Appetite and Scope](./01-appetite-and-scope.md).
- `baseline` — what users have today, against which a reduced version is compared. Rules:
  [01 — Appetite and Scope](./01-appetite-and-scope.md).
- `circuit breaker` — the default of cancelling rather than extending unfinished work. Rules:
  [01 — Appetite and Scope](./01-appetite-and-scope.md).
- `core` — the non-negotiable outcome a unit of work guarantees. Rules:
  [01 — Appetite and Scope](./01-appetite-and-scope.md).
- `downhill` — the phase in which unknowns are solved and only execution remains. Rules:
  [01 — Appetite and Scope](./01-appetite-and-scope.md).
- `EARS` — the Easy Approach to Requirements Syntax, a constrained assertion template. Rules:
  [02 — Plan and Slices](./02-plan-and-slices.md).
- `Governed by` — the slice heading naming every source a work session loads. Rules:
  [02 — Plan and Slices](./02-plan-and-slices.md).
- `plan zone` — the second-axis zone holding what the project builds next and what bounds it. Rules:
  [00 — The Plan Zone as a Second Axis](./00-plan-zone-as-a-second-axis.md).
- `pre-production gate` — the rule preventing specifications or outside ADRs before the current slice
  is implemented. Rules: [02 — Plan and Slices](./02-plan-and-slices.md).
- `remainder` — negotiable scope beyond the core, ordered so the least valuable is cut first. Rules:
  [01 — Appetite and Scope](./01-appetite-and-scope.md).
- `revision` — a recorded change to a slice's `Goal`, `Core`, `Appetite`, or `Acceptance` after work
  started. Rules: [02 — Plan and Slices](./02-plan-and-slices.md).
- `scope hammering` — forcefully questioning a design or use case to finish inside the budget. Rules:
  [01 — Appetite and Scope](./01-appetite-and-scope.md).
- `slice` — one vertical, demonstrable unit of work bounded by an appetite. Rules:
  [01 — Appetite and Scope](./01-appetite-and-scope.md).
- `uphill` — the phase in which unknowns or unsolved problems remain. Rules:
  [01 — Appetite and Scope](./01-appetite-and-scope.md).
